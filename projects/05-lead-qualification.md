# AI Lead Qualification and Follow-up

**Problem:** A service business gets form leads of mixed quality; good ones wait days for a reply.

**Solution:** Each new form submission is scored by Claude (budget, urgency, fit) and routed: hot leads alert Slack instantly and get a personalised first email, others go into a nurture list. Unanswered hot leads trigger a reminder after 24 hours.

**Stack:** n8n · Webhook / Typeform / Google Forms · Claude · Google Sheets or HubSpot · Slack · Gmail
