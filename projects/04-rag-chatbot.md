# RAG Chatbot on Company Documents

**Problem:** Staff and customers ask the same policy and product questions; answers are buried in PDFs and docs.

**Solution:** An n8n AI Agent with Claude answers from the company's own documents only, cites the source section, and says "I don't know" instead of guessing. Available on a website widget or Telegram.

**Stack:** n8n AI Agent · Claude · vector store (Supabase/Qdrant or n8n in-memory for demo) · Telegram / n8n Chat
