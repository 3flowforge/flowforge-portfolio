# Company & Services

## Overview

LayerLab 3D is an online 3D printing service (fictional demo company). We print parts from customer files using three technologies: FDM (filament, best for low-cost prototypes and large parts), SLA (resin, best for fine detail and smooth surfaces) and SLS (nylon powder, best for strong functional parts with no support marks). We also offer design-for-printing help, file repair, and post-processing (support removal, sanding, priming, painting, dyeing). Orders are placed by uploading a file and choosing material, quantity and finish.

## Contact and hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 (GMT+7). Email support@layerlab3d.example. Quotes for custom finishing, very large parts or more than 100 pieces are prepared by a human within one business day.
