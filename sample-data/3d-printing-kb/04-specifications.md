# Specifications

## Tolerances

Typical dimensional accuracy: FDM plus or minus 0.3 mm or 0.5 percent (whichever is larger), SLA plus or minus 0.15 mm, SLS plus or minus 0.3 mm. Tighter tolerances need post-machining and a manual quote.

## Build volume

Maximum part size in one piece: FDM 300 x 300 x 300 mm, SLA 200 x 125 x 220 mm, SLS 165 x 165 x 300 mm. Larger parts can be split, printed in sections and bonded; ask for a manual quote.

## Layer height and infill

FDM layer height 0.1 to 0.3 mm (default 0.2 mm) with 20 percent infill by default; functional parts can use 40 to 100 percent infill at extra cost. SLA layer height 0.025 to 0.1 mm (default 0.05 mm). SLS layer height 0.1 mm, always solid.
