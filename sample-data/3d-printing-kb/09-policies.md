# Policies

## Quality guarantee

If a part has a printing defect caused by us (layer shift, missing features, dimensions outside the stated tolerances), report it with photos within 14 days of delivery and we reprint it free or refund. Design issues such as walls below the minimum thickness are not covered.

## Confidentiality and files

Uploaded files are used only to produce your order and are deleted 90 days after delivery. We sign NDAs on request at no charge.

## Restricted items

We do not print weapons, firearm parts or components, or items that infringe trademarks or copyrights. Printed parts are not certified food safe or medical grade.
