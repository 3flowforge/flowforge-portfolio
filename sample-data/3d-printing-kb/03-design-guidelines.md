# Design Guidelines

## Wall thickness

Minimum recommended wall thickness: FDM 1.2 mm (0.8 mm absolute minimum), SLA 0.6 mm for supported walls, SLS 0.8 mm. Thinner walls may print but can break during support removal or shipping.

## Overhangs and supports

FDM can print overhangs up to about 45 degrees from vertical without supports; steeper overhangs and bridges longer than about 10 mm need supports, which leave marks. SLA always uses supports, placed on the least visible side when possible. SLS needs no supports at all.

## Holes, clearances and text

Holes printed vertically in FDM come out slightly undersized; design them 0.2 to 0.3 mm larger or ask us to drill them. Clearance between moving parts: FDM 0.4 mm, SLA 0.2 mm, SLS 0.5 mm. Embossed or engraved text should be at least 0.6 mm deep and 4 mm tall for FDM; SLA can go down to 2 mm tall.

## Hollow parts

Hollow resin parts must have at least two drain holes of 3 mm or more so uncured resin can escape. Hollow SLS parts need escape holes of at least 5 mm to remove unsintered powder.
