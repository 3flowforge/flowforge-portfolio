# Delivery

## Production time

Standard production time after order confirmation: FDM 2 to 3 business days, SLA 3 to 4 business days, SLS 5 to 7 business days. Post-processing adds 1 to 2 business days.

## Shipping

Domestic standard shipping 2 to 4 business days, express 1 to 2 business days. International shipping available to most countries, 5 to 10 business days. Tracking is emailed when the parcel leaves.
