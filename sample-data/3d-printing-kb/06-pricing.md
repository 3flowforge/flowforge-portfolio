# Pricing

## How pricing works

Price = material volume x material rate + 10 USD setup fee per order, with a 25 USD minimum order. Rates per cubic centimeter: PLA 0.12, PETG 0.15, ABS or ASA 0.18, TPU 0.25, standard resin 0.35, tough resin 0.45, Nylon PA12 0.40 USD. Example: a 50 cm3 part in PETG costs 50 x 0.15 + 10 = 17.50, so the 25 USD minimum applies.

## Discounts and extras

Quantity discount: 10 percent for 10 or more identical parts, 20 percent for 50 or more. Sanding and primer: plus 30 percent. Painting and color matching: manual quote. Rush production (FDM only, 24 to 48 hours): plus 50 percent.
