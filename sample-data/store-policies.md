# PawHaven Pet Store — Policies (fictional)

## Shipping
Orders ship within 2 business days. US standard shipping takes 4–7 business days and is free over $49. Express (1–3 days) costs $12.

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days. Damaged items: send a photo within 7 days of delivery for a free replacement or full refund. Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns.

## Products
Cat trees support cats up to 9 kg per platform. Beds come in S (up to 4 kg), M (up to 7 kg) and L (up to 12 kg). All scratching posts use natural sisal.

## Wholesale
Wholesale starts at 50 units per SKU with 30% off retail. Contact wholesale@pawhaven.example.
